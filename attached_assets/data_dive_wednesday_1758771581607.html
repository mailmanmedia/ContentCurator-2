But What About...

"Liverpool Won With 11 Changes"

Against Championship opposition. Try 11 changes against Real Madrid and see what happens. Context matters.

"Young Players Need Experience"

True, but not in crucial matches. Development happens in training and cup early rounds, not European knockout games.

"Klopp Did It Successfully"

Klopp had different personnel and rarely rotated this heavily. Plus, even he struggled with depth in crucial seasons.

The Bottom Line

Liverpool's "depth" is an illusion created by early-season cup games against weak opposition. When October's fixture congestion hits, when injuries mount, when rotation becomes necessity rather than luxury - that's when the myth dies. The data doesn't lie: this squad has stars and starters, but precious little in between.
