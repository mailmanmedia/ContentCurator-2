3. Fixing Placement and Snap-To-Grid Bugs (from Audit)
Overlay position bounding: Make sure your snap-to-grid logic constrains overlays so they cannot be dragged or programmatically placed outside of the presentation/canvas area.

Use a “constrain position” function before saving or setting overlay coordinates.

Example (pseudo-JS):

    Math.max(0, Math.min(y, canvasHeight - height))
  ];
}
